Privacy policy

LED Banner (tubers.ledbanner.ledlight) respects your privacy.


No data collection. The app does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads.


On your device only. Your messages, favorites and settings are saved only on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.


No internet needed. The app works fully offline and does not request internet, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.


Purchases. The optional PRO upgrade is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payments.


Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.


Contact. Questions? Email us at the address shown on our Google Play listing.
